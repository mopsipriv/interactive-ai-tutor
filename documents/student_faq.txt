Student FAQ - AI Tutor Assistant
OAMK Tietotekniikka / Degree Programme in Information Technology

---

Q: What is study right and how long do I have?
A: Study right (opiskeluoikeus) determines how long you can study at the university. For a 240-credit bachelor's degree the standard duration is 4 years, but study right is typically granted for 5 years from your enrollment date. Check your valid_until date in the system using the profile command. If your study right is expiring soon, contact your teacher immediately.

---

Q: How many credits do I need per semester?
A: The degree is 240 credits over 4 years. To stay on track you should complete approximately 60 credits per academic year, or about 30 credits per semester. If you fall significantly below this pace your teacher may flag you as at-risk. Use the plan command to see your current progress and recommended next courses.

---

Q: What is the difference between TVT and DIN programs?
A: TVT2025S-OHJ (Tietotekniikan tutkinto-ohjelma, Ohjelmistokehitys) is taught in Finnish and started in autumn 2025. DIN2025S (Degree Programme in Information Technology) is taught in English and is designed for international students and Finnish students who want to study in English. Both lead to the same Insinööri (AMK) degree of 240 credits over 4 years. The course content is similar but course codes and some course names differ between programs.

Q: What courses do I need to complete before joining the AI Chatbot Development project?
A: For the AI Chatbot Development project you need to complete three prerequisite courses: Johdatus ohjelmointiin (IN00CS84), Tietokannat ja rajapinnat (IN00DL11), and Linux Administration (IN00DU04). You can check your eligibility using the eligibility command in the system.

---

Q: What courses do I need to complete before joining the Web Application Project?
A: For the Web Application Project you need to complete three prerequisite courses: Johdatus ohjelmointiin (IN00CS84), Olio-ohjelmointi ja oliopohjainen suunnittelu (IN00CS88), and Web-sovellusten perusteet (IN00DL12). Use the eligibility command to check if you qualify.

---

Q: What courses do I need to complete before joining the Mobile App Project?
A: For the Mobile App Project you need to complete two prerequisite courses: Olio-ohjelmointi ja oliopohjainen suunnittelu (IN00CS88) and Mobiiliohjelmointi natiiviteknologioilla (IN00CT07). Use the eligibility command to check your status.

---

Q: How do I request enrollment in a course?
A: Use the request command followed by the course name or code. For example: request IN00DL12. Your teacher will review the request and approve or decline it. You can check the status of your requests using the my_requests command.

---

Q: How do I check which courses I have completed?
A: Use the profile command to see your completed courses, ongoing courses, and grades. You can also use the plan command to see your full study plan with progress indicators for each semester.

---

Q: What do the status symbols mean in my study plan?
A: The study plan uses four symbols to show your progress. A checkmark (✅) means the course is completed. A rotating arrow (🔄) means the course is currently ongoing. A clipboard (📋) means the course is planned but not yet started. A cross (❌) means the course is not yet started and may be overdue based on your year of study.

---

Q: What happens if I am identified as at-risk?
A: If you have completed fewer credits than expected for your year of study or have received low grades, your teacher may flag you as at-risk. This means your teacher will contact you to discuss your progress and offer support. You can also proactively use the ask command to get study advice from the AI tutor.

---

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Use the password command in the system. You will be prompted to enter your current password and then your new password twice. Passwords are stored securely using bcrypt hashing.

---

Q: What is the ask command used for?
A: The ask command lets you ask questions about your studies, courses, study right, and program requirements. The AI tutor uses a knowledge base of real course descriptions and program guides to answer your questions. For example: ask What is Linux Administration about? or ask How many credits do I need to graduate?

---

Q: Can I enroll in courses from another program?
A: Some courses are shared between TVT and DIN programs, for example Ammatillinen kehittyminen ja työelämätaidot (YY00DU46), Digi- ja tietotekniset taidot (YY00DU47), English for Working Life (YY00DU52), Svenska för arbetslivet (YY00DU51), Ammattitaidon syventäminen 1 (IN00FM22) and Ammattitaidon syventäminen 2 (IN00FM23). For other cross-program enrollments, contact your teacher.

---

Q: What is practical training (harjoittelu) and when does it happen?
A: Practical training is a mandatory 30-credit component completed in year 4. TVT students complete it under course code IN00CT02 and DIN students under T008130D. During training you work in a real company in your field and apply the knowledge and skills learned during your studies. The placement is agreed individually between you and your teacher.

---

Q: What is the bachelor's thesis (opinnäytetyö) and when do I write it?
A: The bachelor's thesis is a mandatory 15-credit final project completed in year 4. TVT students use course code T009015 and DIN students use T009015D. You choose a purposeful topic, define its scope and objective, compile a theoretical framework, apply research or development methods, and present your results. You work under the supervision of a teacher and must follow ethical and responsible practices.
