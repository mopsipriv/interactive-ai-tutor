PROGRAM OVERVIEW

TVT2025S-OHJ Program Overview
Program: Tietotekniikan tutkinto-ohjelma, Ohjelmistokehityksen suuntautumisvaihtoehto
Code: TVT2025S-OHJ
Degree: Insinööri (AMK)
Credits: 240
Duration: 4 years
Start: Autumn 2025
Language: Finnish

Students graduate with diverse knowledge and skills to work in software development and expert roles in rapidly developing international ICT fields. Studies include programming, software development, web and mobile application development, server programming, IoT software development, and software process management. Students can also study robotics and artificial intelligence. Company-oriented product development projects are an important part of studies where students learn to work in teams and understand the importance of active knowledge seeking.

DIN2025S Program Overview
Program: Degree Programme in Information Technology
Code: DIN2025S
Degree: Insinööri (AMK)
Credits: 240
Duration: 4 years
Start: Autumn 2025
Language: English

The DIN program is taught entirely in English and is designed for international students and Finnish students wishing to study in English. The program covers web development, mobile programming, cloud services, software testing, IoT, data analysis and machine learning. Finnish language courses are included for international students at levels from beginner to working life proficiency. The program combines technical studies with project work and practical training in companies.
